Why Track Your Running & Marathon Training Progress?

Developing new habits is a neurological process. Habit loops depend on reminders, routines, and rewards. Keeping a physical sheet printed out on your desk or wall is an environment-design tactic. It acts as an unavoidable environmental cue that triggers you to perform the habits. The physical action of checking off a box triggers a micro-dose of dopamine, satisfying the reward loop and reinforcing your discipline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is sleep critical for runner recovery?

Growth hormone release peaks during deep sleep cycles. This is when muscle fibers repair, bone density adapts, and energy reserves are replenished.

What counts as cross-training for runners?

Low-impact cardiovascular activities like swimming, cycling, or strength training help build muscle balance without joint impact.

Why is hydration tracking important for runners?

Even mild dehydration of 2% can lead to muscle cramps, increased heart rate, and significant drops in running performance.
