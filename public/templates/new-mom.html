Why Track Your Postpartum & New Mom Self-Care Progress?

Developing new habits is a neurological process. Habit loops depend on reminders, routines, and rewards. Keeping a physical sheet printed out on your desk or wall is an environment-design tactic. It acts as an unavoidable environmental cue that triggers you to perform the habits. The physical action of checking off a box triggers a micro-dose of dopamine, satisfying the reward loop and reinforcing your discipline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a new mom track for herself?

Focus on primary needs: drinking water, eating nutrient-dense meals, sleeping whenever possible, showering, and getting fresh air.

How does walking help postpartum recovery?

Gentle walking boosts blood circulation, decreases postpartum blood clot risks, lifts mood, and provides fresh air.

Why is journaling helpful for postpartum anxiety?

Writing down concerns externalizes thoughts, helps process emotional shifts, and tracks your personal postpartum recovery.
