Why Track Your Mental Health & Self-Care Tracker Progress?

Developing new habits is a neurological process. Habit loops depend on reminders, routines, and rewards. Keeping a physical sheet printed out on your desk or wall is an environment-design tactic. It acts as an unavoidable environmental cue that triggers you to perform the habits. The physical action of checking off a box triggers a micro-dose of dopamine, satisfying the reward loop and reinforcing your discipline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does walking outdoors improve mental health?

Getting natural sunlight and walking in green spaces lowers cortisol levels, regulates your circadian rhythm, and boosts serotonin production.

Why is gratitude journaling effective?

Gratitude retrains your reticular activating system to seek positive events throughout the day rather than focusing on stressors.

How does screen time affect anxiety?

Constant notifications, doomscrolling, and comparing yourself to social media feeds keep the nervous system in a state of high alert, heightening anxiety.
