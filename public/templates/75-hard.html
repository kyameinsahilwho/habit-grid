The 75 Hard Challenge, created by Andy Frisella, is designed to build unwavering discipline, mental resilience, and physical strength. The challenge consists of 5 strict daily rules: follow a diet with no cheat meals or alcohol, complete two 45-minute workouts (one must be outdoors), drink a gallon of water, read 10 pages of non-fiction, and take a progress photo. A single slip-up resets you to Day 1. Keeping a physical habit tracker sheet printed out on your desk or wall is one of the most effective ways to avoid mistakes, keep visual accountability, and see your streak grow.

Why Track Your 75 Hard Challenge Tracker Progress?

Developing new habits is a neurological process. Habit loops depend on reminders, routines, and rewards. Keeping a physical sheet printed out on your desk or wall is an environment-design tactic. It acts as an unavoidable environmental cue that triggers you to perform the habits. The physical action of checking off a box triggers a micro-dose of dopamine, satisfying the reward loop and reinforcing your discipline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do my two 75 Hard workouts back-to-back?

No, the workouts must be separate. It is generally recommended to leave at least 3-4 hours between workouts to respect the challenge rules.

What counts as reading for 75 Hard?

You must read 10 pages of non-fiction, self-help, or educational books. Audiobooks do not count, and fiction is not allowed.

Why is a physical printout better than an app?

A physical sheet on your wall acts as a constant environmental trigger. You cannot swipe it away, snooze notifications, or ignore it. It remains visible as a reminder of your commitment.
